Welcome to LFS International server! ( currently in dev from Zero with NodeJS and InSim )

Our server is designed for players from all around the world!
To make communication seamless, the server can translates messages into your game’s language.
- In the Global channel, click on the text to translate it.

We're currently in development, aiming to create a Cruise / RPG experience.
The goal is to provide engaging gameplay without being overly punishing for new players.

Rules of the Road:
 - We drive on the right side of the road.
 - Avoid unnecessary collisions with other vehicles.
 - Don’t abuse excuses—we know you ''didn’t do it on purpose''!

The team comprises just one person, so there's no need to rush.
All help is welcome, race layouts, your ideas..

Join us, have fun, and enjoy the ride!
